What this is, and what it is not
  • Model: 24 mm wide 2-D tile, steady conduction, constant conductivities (W 130, Cu 380 W/m·K), coolant at 100 °C. Hot designs are therefore optimistic for tungsten.
  • Limits are screening values (Cu interface 450 °C, where CuCrZr starts to lose strength; W surface 1300 °C, recrystallisation), not design-code allowables. Move them with the sliders.
  • The surrogate's mean error on held-out designs is about 1–2 °C; its nominal 95 % bands are slightly over-confident (87–88 % coverage), so amber cells mean "check with the real model".
  • Thermal only. The stress at the W/Cu joint, especially after cool-down from joining, is a separate and often harder limit.
